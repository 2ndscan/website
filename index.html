Why it matters

How much of what you think did you actually decide?

  • Your feed chooses for you

    Apps learn what keeps you scrolling, and show you more of it.

  • Fakes are easy to make

    AI can copy a voice, a face or a news story in minutes.

  • Your circle shapes you

    We pick up views from friends, family and the news, often without noticing.

Curiosity and critical thinking are what make us human. In a world built for reacting, they’re worth practising.

Why 2ndScan

It started with a step back.

“My views have changed over time, through the friends I have, the media I consume and the state of the world. It took a step back to wonder whether they still reflect my values.”

Christina, founder

2ndScan is a free, independent project to help everyone make that step back a habit: on the phone, in the family chat, on the feed and in the news.

The habit

Trace it. Test it. Own it.

Three curious questions for anything you see, hear or believe. When something makes you feel strongly, that’s your cue.

  1. 1Trace it

    Where did this come from?

    Who’s behind it, and what do others say?

  2. 2Test it

    What would change my mind?

    What if the opposite were true?

  3. 3Own it

    Does this fit who I want to be?

    Is it my values talking, or my feed, my friends or my mood?

Use it on

  • The news
  • Social media
  • Family chats
  • AI fakes
  • Scam calls
  • Posts that stir up anger

Money, passwords or panic? Stop, and check another way.

Built on research into considering the opposite, stepping back from a problem and checking what other sources say. Why these three questions

How it works

Learn it. Practise it. Prove it.

2ndScan doesn’t tell anyone what to think. It asks the questions, then leaves the decision with you.

  • Learn

    Free sessions with community groups, libraries, charities and universities.

  • Practise

    A web page that looks like your own feed, chats and messages. Try an example below.

  • Prove

    Research with partners into whether people grow more curious and confident.

Take a second look

Trace it

Which experts? Is anyone else reporting this?

Test it

What evidence would make me doubt it?

Own it

Would I be glad I’d shared this tomorrow?

Roadmap

One habit, growing step by step.

Each phase practises the same three questions on something harder. We only move on when real people show it helps.

  1. Phase 1

    Now

    Build the habit

    Free sessions, a take-away card and the practice tool, starting with scams and AI fakes. Measured with research partners.

  2. Phase 2

    Next

    Question what you’re told

    Rumours, headlines, and health and money claims.

  3. Phase 3

    Later

    Question “us and them”

    Posts that stir up fear or blame, developed with specialist partners.

  4. Phase 4

    Later

    Make it everyday

    Schools, through teachers and parents, and a phone app if people want one.

Our pledge

A tool for society, not a product to sell.

Like a good public library, 2ndScan should be free, shared and there for everyone. We’re writing these promises down now, before any money is involved.

  • Free for everyone

    Not now, and not later.

  • Your thinking stays yours

    No account, no adverts, no data sales and no tracking.

  • No one buys influence

    Funders listed publicly, with no say in what 2ndScan teaches.

  • Open and honest

    Results published, good or bad.

Being set up A not-for-profit company, open-source code and independent evaluation.

If 2ndScan ever has to choose between growth and trust, trust wins.
