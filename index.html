Take a second look.

A free thinking habit for every age. Three simple questions that help you pause, look again and decide for yourself, whether it’s a scam call, a fake clip, a rumour or a hateful post.

Not a scam checker. Not a fact-checker. A thinking habit you keep for life.

Say it “second scan”. No adverts, no account and no tracking.

How it works

Three questions. Any age. Anywhere.

2ndScan doesn’t tell anyone what to think. It asks the questions that help you think things through, then leaves the decision with you.

  1. Who’s saying this, and why?
  2. What might be missing?
  3. What do I really think now?

The questions work at nine and at ninety, on a screen or on the doorstep. The aim is for them to become a habit that goes with you. Success means people need us less.

The one rule

Money, passwords or panic?

Stop. Check it another way, through a number or app you already trust, and talk to someone you trust.

See it in action

One skill, three disguises.

Scams, rumours and hate look different, but they pull the same levers: tricks that push us to act before we think. Pick an example and take a second look.

Choose an example

Phone call · The voice sounds just like him

“Gran, it’s Jamie. I’ve lost my phone and I’m in trouble. Can you send £300 to my friend today? Please don’t tell Mum.”

First instinct Help him, and fast.

Take a second look

Levers it pulls

  • Heartstrings
  • Rush
  • Secrecy

Ask yourself

Could this be an AI copy of his voice? Why would he want it kept secret?

A safe next step

Hang up and call him back on the number you already have. A family code word helps too.

Family group chat · Forwarded many times

“SHARE before they delete this! Doctors don’t want you to know about this one simple cure.”

First instinct Warn everyone I know.

Take a second look

Levers it pulls

  • Rush
  • Too good to be true
  • Only you know the truth

Ask yourself

Where’s the evidence, and who’s behind it? Who gains if I share it?

A safe next step

Don’t share it yet. Ask a pharmacist or GP, or check the NHS website.

Local community page · Shared 12,400 times

“BREAKING: Huge fire in the market square tonight. We all know who’s behind this. Share before they delete it!”

First instinct Feel angry, and share it.

Take a second look

Levers it pulls

  • Us and them
  • Fear
  • Rush

Ask yourself

Who exactly is “they”? Who benefits if I feel angry?

A safe next step

Don’t share yet. Check local news and police updates first.

Invented examples, not live checks. A lever means slow down and check. It doesn’t always mean fake.

Why a habit

Why a habit, not another app?

Banks, phone companies and charities are doing good work to protect people. But each one protects its own customers, in its own app, for as long as it runs. A habit goes wherever you go.

  • Works without a screen

    At the door, on the phone and at the kitchen table.

  • Never out of date

    The tricks keep changing. The questions still work.

  • Yours for life

    Once you’ve learned it, it’s yours to keep, and to pass on to the people you love.

Tools change. Tricks change.
A habit stays with you.

That’s how 2ndScan can stay free for everyone, forever.

Our pledge

A tool for society, not a product to sell.

Like a good public library, 2ndScan should be free, shared and there for everyone. We’re writing these promises down now, before any money is involved.

  • Free for everyone, forever

    No charge to use 2ndScan. Not now, and not later.

  • Your thinking stays yours

    No account, no sign-up and nothing to hand over.

  • No adverts, no data sales, no tracking

    Your attention and your data are not the business model.

  • Funders can’t buy influence

    Every funder listed publicly, with no say in what 2ndScan tells people.

Being set up A not-for-profit company, open-source code and independent evaluation, published good or bad.

If 2ndScan ever has to choose between growth and trust, trust wins.
