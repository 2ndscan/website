# Sources for the figures on 2ndscan.com

Every figure on the site links to its source. This file records the detail behind each one, so it can be re-checked and updated.

**Last checked:** 28 October 2026. When you update a figure, update this date and the "checked in October 2026" lines in `index.html`.

## Figures used on the landing page

| On the site | Source | Detail |

Combined N = 37,025. |
| A different view | [Modirrousta-Galian and Higham (2023)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36996156/) | *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General*. The debate is open, which is why the site says 2ndScan should be tested independently. |

## Figures from the concept document that are not on the site, and why

- **Fraud incidents from the latest Crime Survey for England and Wales (ONS).** The newest figure couldn't be confirmed from more than one source. Check the [ONS crime bulletins](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/crimeandjustice) directly before using it.
- **"21% drop" after the Bad News game (University of Cambridge, 2020).** Replaced by the larger 2025 review, because the 2023 reanalysis questions what that figure measures.
- **"Structurally fragile"** (quoted from the DSIT research in the concept document). This phrase couldn't be found in the published sources. Check the full report before quoting it.
- **35% of children say chatbots feel like talking to a friend** (Internet Matters). Found only in secondary coverage, not confirmed in the report itself.
- **Facebook users over 65 shared seven times as many fake news articles** (US data from 2016). Old and not UK data.
- **Hate and extremism figures** (Ministry of Justice 2022, Home Office and MI5 2024). Accurate, but left off the landing page to keep the tone calm and non-political. They remain in the concept document.
